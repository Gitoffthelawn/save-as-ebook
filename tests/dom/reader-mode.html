ARTICLEONE Distillation is the business of deciding which part of a page is the part someone came to read. The heuristic is text density against link density: a paragraph of prose scores well, a list of five links to other stories does not, and the subtree with the best total score wins the page.

ARTICLETWO That heuristic is why furniture disappears. A navigation bar is almost entirely links, a sidebar of related stories is entirely links, and a footer is links and a copyright line. None of them can outscore four paragraphs of prose, so none of them survive into the article that comes back.

ARTICLETHREE It is also why the heuristic cannot be trusted everywhere. A forum thread is prose interleaved with links, an api reference is a wall of short definitions, and a changelog is a list. On those pages a distiller either returns nothing or returns something confidently wrong, and the caller has to be ready for both.
